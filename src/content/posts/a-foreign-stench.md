An indian wrench, how a foreign spark can see two eyes fly. Do till the weave, old man, I can tune the cane, to an eagle, pelican dip, your beak, is not too smooth, for my feathered coat, I can flip, my flute woove, the woven pedestel stem phlegm, a burrow shadow. How deep can you vessel burrow cry, or a heart shape soar - how do you fly? Do find your cave safety crow, I like a fallen ember, eyes open show. Do scratch some more scrawl on your pen shaved tame, I have nine burrows to my name, and your eye work, is sleet, as a child with a withered willow treet.
