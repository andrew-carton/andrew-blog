Holy Water

Two hands and a stone - bitter - how blood can rinse - a sip a day, the flavour known. Why rinse a spit? A pedestal of might, has a foam of delight, a child baptising, a northern blight. A biten cane, a sermon sane - how the eye can till one war together the point woven crying fame. And to till the war, the cult tilled night, a tapestry of protest, on both sides fight? A loom could capture footings rinsed, yet all together, the cry, what town twice shy, the footings loom, a child, a dark foot, and one sermon together swooned? Unity, is an angel stem, and an angle once, why protest, to all stemed hem?
