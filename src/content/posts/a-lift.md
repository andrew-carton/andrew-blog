When God abandoned me, what did he leave, but demon footprints, left within wedlock - a maid, and two entangled chains, the tail a seamstress,  even entwined beamed.


A pluck divine, the hair, the loom, entwined to gaze - to find the harp silenced and hands locked mirrored front gaze, and soul to a maze, to find in ugliness a country, unravelled greed, a witch the island root, and a demon, the power a coin, has a note alone, his hands to a wind ghost alive to praise.

I pluck, half froze, the death winds as sands, and the half effort, the affect, the vibration, a horse, two mares, and a princess bares.
