Surrender to God or the higher powers is not easy, when there is no clear direction. It almost seems like an uphill battle when there is no guidance, that we have to get somewhere, in order to get somewhere else.

I think the first step, in surrender, is not that we need to get anywhere, but taking the writings and seeing that they mean nothing to anybody except me, then that is not true at all really. Writing, expression, does something. It creates a line of manifestations -- they might not be conscious, nor might they be intentional, but they can be honest.

Surrender is noticing, the anxiety, the anguish, that keeps us wanting things to change, because we did something, or didn't do something else, or with big promises, and changes that the current situation is recoverable when it is not at all. Then you realise you have a tool, since child-hood, and it is writing. It can be private, or public, it matters little, start where it is comfortable.

Surrender, the first step, can be simply stopping, and seeing why are coping mechanisms, or things we used to do are no longer working. And often, it is about getting back on track, then that takes time too. Does it take time for God to work?

Sometimes, surrender, can be a track record, of getting through awful times, by just letting go, a little bit at a time, trying something different, something, a little change. A noting, an embrace, an aligning of intents. Even just stepping into a church, and dipping your hands into the bowl, and blessing yourself. What souls are in there, are they going through difficult times too? The Sunday mass, can be good times, to take a dip in. Sometimes, the best clothed individuals, know that they are there, because they uplift people, that they are comfortable in whatever they are wearing. The throngs or crowds or rows of people together, the silent couple, the elderly men or women, cluttered throughout. It's not really difficult to see, where silent congregations come and go. I do not know, but Archangel Michael showed me something one Sunday. That I was loved, by just stepping in and stepping out, that the place was built for me, that everyone was there because of love, and that was nice too. That everyone can be lonely and be situated in love.

The big change about surrender, I believe happens when we start noticing. We notice love in all the places in our life, that need a torch, and often shining a torch on where love is not, can be a great stepping stone in surrender. Where are the places, that love needs to be looked through, or where it is lacking? And often those who do spiritual work, are in gratitude, or those, quite often like myself, are in anxiety and are in anguish.

At the moment, I am dealing with great anxiety, and I do not know how to solve it. I could play a computer game, I suppose, watch something or other, do something productive, but I feel empty. Nothing works.

I just feel anxiety, and put on music, and then I try and surrender or do something about it, but realise, sitting there feeling anxiety, isn't really the solution. 

Then, I realise, I can create another blog article, and that helps me. This stupid blog article is helping me, as I write it. The whole blog column stinks, but it's my self-help solution, when nothing else has worked. Not thousands and thousands on therapies, on paid or free for, of medications, of interventions, mindless jobs, that did not take me anywhere, because there wasn't pathways of love, around them.

I see, that my life, has been a tragedy, when looked from other people's abstract victories (which I think they are). Then, I look at it, and I see I am a King, on the top of a mountain. One more blog article, isn't going to get me to the top, but it releaves the anxiety.

Surrender isn't the big gesture to God, that gets one immediately to the top of the mountain. It's the infinite sips of wisdom or experience that gets one to the bottom. And sometimes, that can be alcohol. The amount of alcohol I sipped to get to the bottom, and the amount of desperate pleas for help, that never made it to God's throne.

Then, I realised something, that nobody might care about my blog articles or my blog, but I do. The little sips on the way, if someone showed me, that it wasn't the way, maybe they were the silent desperations, that are being repeated, where now I have a voice, and a blog, and a way out of it.

The silent sips. What is an alcoholic, but God, sipping the spirits. The tough swallow, that looking at other people's lives, and being ungrateful, for what one has.

And so surrender perhaps, is an invitation, to take the sip off God, and start sipping the spirits other people have. The tough times, where nobody is there, where the sip of alcohol isn't the answer.

It's lying in bed, and saying "I surrender God" or holding onto God in silence for as long as possible, then seeing where it goes.

But that is not it either. God can be found in prayer yes, and in meditations, but God, can found in writings most importantly too.

Open your notebook on your laptop, open your mobile phone. It should be full of prayers. I opened mine and saw it was full of experiences, of God, grasping, at something, that wasn't really there, for a year now. I thought it was just lousy mental health, but I realise it is just human.

You have a notepad, get writing. It might feel awful getting feelings and thoughts onto paper. And anxiety oozing from desperate attempt of inner contempt and realisations, that one is not worthy, to experience, a free flow of guilt free, realisations of life.

Perhaps, one thing, I am grateful for, is the opening to gratitude. When the big anxiety hits, I know it's not an opening or invitation to prayer to bless it away or try and argue it away. I know the feeling, the anxiety, can't be remedied easily, but writing sure helps.

It helps because it gets something on paper, even just to a piece of music.

I know it's difficult. I know life is tough. We look at other people, and see they have it easy, but do they? And then you realise, even if they do have it easy, or if they have it tough, how do I get my contribution in to being helped or helping?

The torch helps.

Try this exercise. What is in your room in this moment, as you read this? Is it a room full of tools, or just idle instruments, lying around. Perhaps, it's time for a clean up, time for a little bit of reflection. 

What do I have, that helps me? What is bothering me? What is steering me in the pathways that help me?

Like, I said, the walk is the one thing that helps. Have an out of town, walk, somewhere like my graveyard. I did it today again, the walk, and it felt good.

Perhaps, I need another walk, to a different place? Or just another activity? Will it be a computer game, I loved playing in the past, or another blog article? Or just a piece of music or a Netflix movie, we signed up for?

It is kind of weak. We have so many distractions, but what etches at the mind -- that we have everything -- but removing everything like I did, until I had nothing but sitting in bed.

Then, I realised, it was a settling, my mind, or me coming to rest, that I could eventually see, that anxiety really is. I do not know what anxiety really is.

I do not know what being spiritual is, but what if that is a higher calling, I had or once had, that I need to acknowledge or integrate into my life too?

The judgements, like Jesus, sweeping through, all the stuff in life, to see what is sacred, or a dishonouring of what was sacred. Is God sacred in your life? How do you find time, to invite God or the higher powers in?

Or must God or the higher powers take everything from me too? And leave me with a crushing hole, where the whole needs to be full, full of writings, and blessings?

And what blessings would you start with? The lack or the addition? The whole or the hole?

Is it time to strip away or just be accountable? I do not know. I do not know why the anxiety is there, but my notes you can now find them online, as I describe what it is.

Perhaps, the journey up Mount Sinai was as it was. Just a journey in love, or a love prior, that found the mountain waiting, that was already climbed.

Or is the divine spot, the little abandoned church and graveyard. The little walk, I can rest in peace. I found it on a blessed compass with directions.

Or perhaps, a second walk, for the day, I can still get another one in? Or, hmm, I see a guide in my sight, checking the time, on the wrist watch I never had. 

I have gifts, spiritual gifts too. Sometimes, I can see in my sight, things happening prior to them happening, or hear a voice telling me something, prior to it happening or seeing something. But, then I forget about them, saying it's just mental health symptoms, but they are not. They are memories of hope, where these mental health symptoms, they might be blessed gifts. Just gifts that people never know how to follow the guidance, like I, where seeing the full length, the desperation sigh, and knowing where I lie, the depth I can never tire be.

When one sees the spiritual gifts, one knows they are not gifts, but plays of consciousness, or something tugging at one, maybe not gifts at all, but just guidance that is being acknowledged.

Like today, on the way home from the graveyard, I tapped the black pointed gate on the way out, and then I felt in my emotions, a big steering wheel, like on a sailor's ship, and it sailed me with the sight, all the way home.

What is lonely? The little guide that adds the funny gifts spiraling things up and away, around the anchors of the form? Some would call be crazy, but the gifts are there, to be confirmed or not, or just found in amusement, then forgotton, not really known to be gifts at all, just tugs on memory, or just little spinning amusements.

A little wicked, how a new guide can show one the new turf. The plays of old emotions, how there isn't enough time, to work through the old guide's difficult coping patterns, and to pull out something of self-acknowledgement or worth. Perhaps, the old guide, is a wave in the distance, in the sight. Is the new guide worthy?

The pause, the extra gifts do find amusement. The two hands held, three times pulsing find, or the eyes flashing on the page, the question blind? You see these gifts, the tears reckon night.

Hands help in the midnight hour. Are you there? And not a word of emotion, just hands pulsing find. Who am I? A midnight guide on the tiring mission flight?

Perhaps, there is the excitement. Not to look to God, or high powers, but simply a guide.

So, let this be your new mantra - "Are you there?". And the response, wait until the hands pulse and pulse.
