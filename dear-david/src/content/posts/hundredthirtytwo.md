Eternity can fly by in an external breath. How one breath flies, when others sighs. How can it be so easy to let God have it all, then twist and turn, and complain you have something? Is it two tears and a child on the winds? A toy for five minutes, and then lunch?

It is the easiest meditation, how feathers ease, the movement the form with tease, and nothing else, but I, with worries on the winds, and a pull away, is it a hand me back, and tears with sins?

How a frown looks, to see all is well, and motion the tears uptell. Or a put-down or pocket but forget until later? Or a pull-away and let me see? Pop it on the wall, BREATHING, how noticing turns the wall -- how big do you want the sign? Do I not do well, until it is your turn? 

What angel do you want? Archangel Michael, the top dog, or is he the smallest shuffle in the toolkit? How, I sigh, when the time is over, how children quick to pry, but shuffle stem, I too, do enlighten the gem.

Notice the shift, it is just Archangel Michael shifting it back, not onto something or someone else - you can have the mask too if you wish?

Who are you? Archangel Michael with a shew, or just a breath aligned with grace and a state, how tears remember, the upwelling face.

How tears others who do not have a child to fly, yet memories others, how I try. Often, the gift exclaim a softening of grace to blame. How one name altered can face a shame, or just one name angered at birth a show? Or a little reminder how breath, can tease a sigh or sign- a brightness show?

Breath - it is so complicated isn't it? How wanting everything, and my two hands tears -- just anything? Or everything again, I will show you how. How tears in paradise, a thank you - and what is forgotten.
