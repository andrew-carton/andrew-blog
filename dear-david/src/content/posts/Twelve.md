Dear David,

How a blacksmith, does lieth, in awakened, frost, the chill, how permission, a foreign bill, does chilling tides, the heart capture, winds lept, yet eager, how a blacksmith coin, the frost hoof dwell, and tidy sums, do coins, blacken gums, yet fawns do escape lies, permission, healing, a frosty dwelling, timed response, a cane, flipped, remake? How humour does breath long tidings finalities sums, yet the cane, I do dwell, fixed, motion, do tell, the ending finality, sworn, findings sum deep, I do share, ears open fleet? Footprints, I do share, a coin weep, flying eye caring aloof, surprise, journeys lit spun? Or a chariot, darkness, is it a window open, the moon, I shy, keep, soul weep, too, and the eye lit scold, comfort, a swallow old? How far reaching holds, do ceilings bridge might, yet trade-links burned, a dwelling lost keep, is it sheep tonight, a shed wept lent? Or the frost, the open air, a point hammered coin feast, dwelled dwelt ned? How positions do chime loft, anger soft, yet the final word, do loom, a chilling chariot, abroad, let, my tidings sworn soul fawl? How barter pieces, do trade even, yet solder, the web deceit, I do lie, high a loom, protection, a snap light, two, I do woo lie extreme, no permission, finality sum, tidings weep, to knowledge, the wisdom claw others, discovery I do caw, no finality, two horns, shappened, a passage kneed, I do the hands saw, too? Or the fright, how nothing, but bight, the bitten loom, nothing this life, too soon, does capture, one foot, I did saw? 



~
