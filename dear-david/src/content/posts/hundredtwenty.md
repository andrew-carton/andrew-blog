Dear David,


Eyes up tilt, paradise Moses wilt, a frown or stair, to follow footing pair
Or a strand on mission light a thread, uphill sent
And my crown light rent.
So, I have arrived one beacen slime hid crime
Feast my eyes, my paths all healed time
Or fashion a mission a thread despair
To death of all, and a landing all light downward care?
