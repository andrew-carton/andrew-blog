Dear David,

Russel Grant - on old cane for an old boy, a health inspection, and a  bit of pulse, twice shy, or a lick of evidence, and a full creme doughnut, on a walk as the crow flies? Get your pathways sorted, the gay cane, is a shame, in this country. Got to get the cane sorted, hospital wide, give me a chariot, and I'll plot some reflection.
