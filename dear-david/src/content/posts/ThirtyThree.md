Dear David,

How I pluck, the forbidden art, the prophecy fame, the cane allowance, in pride, my mission is it six, in patience my flame, in head tilt pride to sow?

Yet how tilt high, the fifth, how to master, in a priest to show, how tidings weep, a girl to glide, till the right hand, and glide the floor, in footings beat, and show me the cane, like a proud husband, slain feet?

Yet what cane, to solder webs a grain, the hand out a husband, slaughtered to a T-bone a hound, flashed grow, to solder mow? Flesh the beast first, the fifth held toe, how a coin, a ring, can find the hindered motion, a sixth tongue beaten flap remark?

Find a pill to shame, the tilt to name, is it I, the second Andrew, yet the shame, a daughter, to another perfume stain, in disappointment tame?

Why alter the web trace beat, fly to the foreign country, with hand held high to fleet, and border chance, a foreign tongue held crime, how responsible thou art, to two visas, and one town a chime, yet a drip to time?

Often weave, a soiled retreat, how two visas, one criminal has a southern tongue, to soldered creeps to one name meet?

And folden care, the moon with pair, do tide the coin, in a pathways, I solder, to one hospital, I do greet in hands worth fair.

A mid-husband, my eye, in prophecy flame, just a tilt, and head, spun shy, and master the cane, like a nomad's fly, or shelter weave, one name grain, one in disappointment, or two, in mercy, all children, a hospital flirt local grain?


I fold a weave, death sieve tame, how the cane I fly, in youth correction light, yet solder ease, how hospitals tease, I will leave soiled understandings, in a spread of hospitals, with a trail of blood, a hound, the web trace knee for thee, and a flower the ending, a dead child, with a cough, a hound exhales to grow, how shelter ease, two knees be, and death a path, do find one hospital two in company flight, in ease, the blood tilt crop, the moon sun shy, and the trail of responsbility, to a closed hospital, in the death a child opened cane flirt, to a man two tables, and a room, shadow girth.
