You see a map. On the map is an X. The X marks you and your location. You and the location are the same.

The map is neither on you or in your possession. You do not need to remember the last place you were at. Just look at the map, in your mind, and see the X, and see red dotted lines, taking you from X to X, in a loop or as complicated as you like, until the X remains, and the path closes. 

This visualisation was given by a guide. 

You can get sophisticated with the meditation. You can just see an X in front of you, and then breathe. Or you can see a flashing X that is red to say you are here.

If you want to add extras you can. You can imagine two pairs of hands, one pair imaginary, opening up a big scroll, and furnishingng a big map, with X, on it, to say you are ready.

And then scroll the map back up as you imagine you are here.

Then, if you are using the scroll, if you like the technique, pull out the scroll labelled Gratitude. And just find the X tailored beneath it. You do not even have to place it correctly. Or you can see the X, move around the Gratitude in a circle and slowly vanish.

Visualisation is fun. 

Imagine Gratitude on the scroll then and an X beneath it. How I would love to proof of a spiritual guide, or get to know one? How would I know one guide is present.

Place a question mark after the X?

Hmm, all of a sudden Gratitude got crossed out and a question mark placed on it too?

It is funny how the mind works, or is the question mark crossed out too?

How we need guides in this day and age? Wouldn't it be wonderful a guide with a name? Or would you like something a little more interesting?

You see the guidance is just the X or is it gratitude? Is gratitude to guidance the same?

This is how guides work. If you want to open the door to one, then it is simple. You need triggers, or cues.

Here is another one for Archangel Michael.
"I am here, and that is all".

That is what I have learned about spiritual guides. Sometimes, its the subtlest of impressions, that give guides their gifts. The little visualisations, or the flickers. It's not the big visualisations, the most darking meditations, the 112 dala lama mantras. Guides can surprising. I even have a silly rhyme and trance I use.

You can even drift into a visualisation. Imagine a candle wick placed beside the map on a solid table.

Or imagine an old guide with a lantern lost in a maze, and his lantern flashes like a watchtower across your eyes in a line.

I have silly cues.

But use the verbal ones if you want. The visual ones might take some time.
