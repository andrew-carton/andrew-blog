A bit of gay cane in youth, sieve the humour like a rumour, a bit of eye work for a northern boy? Or a quiz of eye put downs, or a whip like a cruiser on a gone off playboy?

Fix your pathways, to something respectable, not a gone off, half round table. If you didn't hear the first slur, you aren't in for the full purr.

I can sieve a cane, with a tilt and grow, a little inquiry, and a full house, with a script and tips?

Sell me something new, not some eye candy, with glasses and a bill shop pend.
