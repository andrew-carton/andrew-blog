How coth, a man now child -- and dead -- why fought? Greed, two wings, yet never sings, the vessel severed, and all dead -- greed - a child woven sled, on flappings stings! Spit, the journey complete, do not nurse a child to a stolen cart flings, the witch dead, stolen head, and spit on all - my journey, sealed - children hearts favoured heads! Woven kind, pick a child, not a stain, my navel caught blight, teh child I Will choose, all pathways, the sailor, why children thought?
