Allow, the smudge, eye, tilled, war, I shall breathe sigh. The bark, the lark, the edge, sofftened, yet teared, wear, care, the touch, sheltered, yet withered, worn, a bird room sown? A glance, uphill, bent, the tweet, the mistake, and a shatter anger, hid backflown find, the memory, gaze, the chirp tease, yet even placed -- a loft in peace, in solitude, the shadow rewind, a word -- and back again -- is it a mistake, my shuffle head, upbeat care? To loom the hand, the withered beak, the right arm greet, and spirit shadow, woven pair, returned, and the flight, two arms, withered, my name, a gasp, on the wind, and what find thee, in flown eye peak -- a fragrant touch, mistakes hid shame, the bark the tears, sheltered -- and now rain?
