How a worn rag, can beat an old man's legs, the chains old youth hair hay, do layer an eye to another beat. I have one side in rags, and the other to a purse, to a name, rewind top fill. Shy my name to a halo, a corridor up and down, the blight, pill, and I will refill, the wings tame, or do you wish my old age, the left peak, eye glitter, the mad hatter, the hood I flown to show scale gain?

How the heart can weave the full corridor, I, Madonna, to a pearl, and two nights, with a spit, and stick grill. Rewind the witch, her clench is a twitch, in youth to clay, find something without an old man trance, or a newbie stance, why butcher a young girl with a chance?
